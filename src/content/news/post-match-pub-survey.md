Over a five-week study with a sample size of 16 (n=12 once you account for the inevitable hammy puller), we ranked every viable post-match pub on the Sunshine Coast.

1. **The Caloundra Hotel** — incumbent. Decent schnitty. The sticky carpet is a feature, not a bug.
2. **The Beerwah Hotel** — only viable when away at Beerwah. Counter meal carries it.
3. **Kings Beach Tavern** — sea breeze. Unfortunately also walking distance, which Pirelli describes as "a problem".
4. **The Brightwater Hotel** — too modern. Won't put on the football. Marked down.
5. **Wherever Westy is taking us "for one quick drink"** — pending further investigation.
