The Boss debuted the diamond. It worked. Right up until the 89th minute, when it did not.

Number Ten put us 1–0 up just before the hour mark with a finish that was, frankly, *belle*. The diamond pinged the ball around the middle third with the kind of crisp triangulation usually reserved for teams that train more than once a week. Glasshouse looked confused. We looked composed. The post-match pub looked imminent.

And then, on 89 minutes, a long throw, a flick-on, a poor first touch from a defender who shall not be named in this report (it's in the squad chat), and 1–1.

Le Croissant took it well. The Boss took it less well. The whiteboard has been re-colour-coded.
