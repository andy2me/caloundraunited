A scrappy 3–1 over Coolum Beach in conditions that can only be described as "Sunshine Coast in May", which is to say: 24 degrees, a four-knot easterly, and a pitch that had been mowed by someone with a personal grudge against straight lines.

## First half

Caloundra started on the front foot. Twomey opened the scoring on 11 minutes, picking up a loose ball at the edge of the box after Coolum's centre-back attempted what could charitably be described as a clearance. 1–0 to the visitors. The skipper celebrated like he'd just won the Champions League, which felt, in context, generous to himself.

Pajic doubled the lead from a corner that, the bench would like to formally state, was absolutely deliberate. He nodded home from six yards, then proceeded to claim he'd "called for it at the near post". Footage to follow.

Coolum pulled one back just before half-time through a penalty that Hollywood went the right way for and saved with his face. The rebound, sadly, fell to a Coolum striker who was still onside by a margin we'll generously round up to "fine".

## Second half

The second half was, as Le Croissant noted post-match, *"plus défensif"*. We dropped deep, soaked up pressure, and got out on the counter through Speedy Ravi, who beat his man over five metres and then promptly ran out of legs. Granty, for his sins, kept overlapping until the 87th minute.

Twomey made it 3–1 from a Granty cutback in injury time. A scrappy finish off his shin, but he's claiming it was "always the plan to bring it down with my standing leg".

## Other notes

- Tank won every header. *Every* header.
- The Tyre played the full 90, no incidents, dependable as advertised.
- Burger's celebration after his last-ditch block in the 78th minute has been formally added to the disciplinary log.
- The oranges were, as ever, the best thing about half-time.

Onwards to Noosa Buderim at home, Saturday week.
