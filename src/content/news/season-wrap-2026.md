## Season Wrap: Caloundra United Look Back on 2026

Another season down, another set of knees that'll never quite be the same. The 2026 campaign is officially retired to the ladder, and it's time to look back on a year that had more ice packs than clean sheets, but never once ran short on heart.

The side finished 6th of 8, wrapping up 14 rounds with 4 wins, 1 draw and 9 losses — 13 points, 32 goals for, 39 against. Not the finals run we were chasing, but every win, every draw, and every gutsy performance came from a group of blokes who kept turning up on a Friday night, kept working for each other, and kept the Caloundra United jersey worth pulling on — Voltaren and all.

For the record, the numbers had some help. The away kit provided a well-documented tailwind all season, and Nige's turmeric juice did its bit for recovery — though even liquid gold couldn't fully offset the headwind of Aaron and Mark shipping out mid-season for a World Cup holiday. Marginal gains, marginal losses, it evens out.

The back end of the season summed it up nicely: a gritty 1–1 draw away to Buderim Power in round 13, then a hard-fought 0–2 loss to Coolum Hammers to close it out. Not the fairytale finish anyone scripted, but an honest reflection of a team that competed to the final whistle — and the final beer afterwards.

Up top, it went down to the wire. Stella Swans and Buddina United both finished on 35 points, with Stella Swans taking top spot on goal difference (+56 to +47) before beating Buddina United 2–1 in the Grand Final on 29 August to be crowned 2026 premiers. Good on them — somebody has to win the thing.

Thank you to every player, coach, volunteer and long-suffering partner who made 2026 a season worth being part of. On to 2027 — same team, fresh strapping.

### Special Mentions

Two blokes earn a special mention as the season closes out.

**Gavin Nunn** has finally hung up the boots after 8 years and well over 100 matches in the Caloundra United shirt — proof that a good pair of knees, or at least a well-managed bad pair, can outlast almost anything. Thank you, Gavin. Enjoy the rest, the extra hour back on a Friday night, and we'll see you on the sideline with a beer in hand.

**Baz Sowerby**, a great teammate the last two seasons, is graduating to Over 45s for 2027 — the natural next step in every over-35s career. We'll miss him in this side, but he's not going far, and he's still very much part of the Caloundra United family. All the best, Razzle Bazzle — may your new age bracket be kinder to your hamstrings than this one was.
