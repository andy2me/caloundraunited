Four different goalscorers, four different celebrations of varying restraint. Caloundra ran out 4–2 winners at Mountain View Reserve in what The Boss described, with characteristic understatement, as "a statement win".

Goals from Twomey, Pajic, Number Ten, and a Granty header that nobody saw coming, including Granty.
