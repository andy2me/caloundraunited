## Match Report

A cool Sunshine Coast evening provided the perfect backdrop for football, but the result was far from ideal as Caloundra United suffered a third consecutive league defeat, going down 5–3 to Buderim Power at Fishermans Road. The loss is a significant blow to United's season ambitions, with the side now bumped outside the top four and facing a difficult climb back into contention.

### United Slip Out of Top Four Despite Jared Hat-Trick

#### Key Moments

Buderim drew first blood in the 5th minute, but Caloundra responded emphatically. Jared levelled matters at the 10-minute mark with a cool finish at the back post, Aaron using his pace to get in behind to the byline with a smart cross — a Caloundra masterclass. United doubled the lead on 20 minutes with Brackley providing Jared a sneaky little assist in a crowded penalty box. Jared completed a superb first-half hat-trick on the half-hour mark — again set up by Mark, this time a Kevin De Bruyne-esque cross on a plate — giving United a commanding 3–1 lead at the break.

> Three goals in twenty minutes — Jared's hat-trick was a masterclass in clinical finishing, and the partnership with Mark was a real highlight of the night.

#### A Second Half to Forget

The second half told a very different story. Buderim Power mounted a relentless comeback, netting on 53, 55, and 63 minutes to turn the match on its head — one of which came from a questionable penalty incident. A yellow card for Aaron on 75 minutes added to the frustration, and a fifth Buderim goal on 83 minutes sealed a deflating defeat.

## Looking Ahead: Coolum Is a Must-Win

Three consecutive defeats and a slip out of the top four means the situation demands an immediate response. The squad must regroup quickly and address the defensive frailties that allowed Buderim to overturn a two-goal deficit so emphatically. Next week's fixture against Coolum is, quite simply, a must-win. The pathway back into the top four remains open, but the margin for error is narrowing. United will need their injured players fit, their combinations sharp, and their collective resolve fully restored.
