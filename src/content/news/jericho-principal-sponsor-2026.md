Caloundra United are delighted to announce [Jericho](https://www.instagram.com/jericho_moffat_beach/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) as the club's principal sponsor for the 2026 season — a partnership that feels less like a business arrangement and more like two local success stories sharing the same values.

Much like Caloundra United, Wes and the team at Jericho are building something special.

What started as a relaxed local venue in the heart of Moffat Beach has quickly become one of the Sunshine Coast's favourite places to gather. Authentic, welcoming, effortlessly cool and never trying too hard — Jericho has built a reputation the old-fashioned way: good people, great hospitality, and creating a place where everyone feels at home.

To be fair, they've also mastered the delicate art of making "just one quiet post-match drink" somehow turn into a three-hour session discussing missed sitters, questionable refereeing decisions and who definitely "still has it."

The support Wes and his team have shown our club has been tremendous from day one. Whether it's backing the club behind the scenes, welcoming players and supporters after matches, or simply creating an environment where our football community can come together, Jericho has become a genuine part of the Caloundra United family.

There's a lot of alignment between what both groups are trying to build.

Caloundra United prides itself on being community-first, inclusive and authentic — a club where football matters, but people matter more. Jericho carries that same energy. Relaxed but ambitious. Professional without losing personality. A venue that feels polished without ever feeling pretentious.

And honestly, after a hard-fought 90 minutes, there are few better places to end up than down at Jericho with teammates, supporters and a cold refreshment in hand.

We're incredibly excited to have Jericho join us as principal sponsor for 2026 and can't wait to continue building something special together — both on and off the pitch.
